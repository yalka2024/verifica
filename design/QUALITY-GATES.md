# Design quality gates

These gates cannot be waived by a design or refinement agent.

- WCAG 2.2 AA automated checks and manual keyboard/screen-reader review
- 320px reflow, mobile, tablet and desktop screenshot checks
- visible focus, reduced motion and non-color status communication
- contrast, touch targets, overflow, loading, empty and error states
- performance budgets, image optimization, font fallback and layout shift
- prohibited-pattern and reference-copy review

Selected direction: **distinctive**
